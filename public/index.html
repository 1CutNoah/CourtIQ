Advanced Stats (NBA.com)

OFF RTGPoints scored per 100 possessions. League avg ≈ 113. Higher is better. Elite = 118+.
DEF RTGPoints allowed per 100 possessions. League avg ≈ 113. Lower is better defense. Elite = 108 or less.
NETOFF RTG minus DEF RTG. The truest measure of team quality. League avg = 0. Above +5 = very good. Above +8 = elite.
PACEPossessions per 48 mins. League avg ≈ 98. Higher pace = more scoring opportunities. Critical for Over/Under bets.
eFG%Effective Field Goal %. Accounts for 3-pointers being worth more. League avg ≈ 53%. Higher = more efficient offense.

Betting Terms

SPREADPoints the favored team must win by. LAL -5.5 means Lakers must win by 6+ for the bet to win.
O/UOver/Under. The total combined score. Bet whether the final score goes over or under this number.
MLMoneyline. Bet on who wins outright. -150 = bet $150 to win $100. +130 = bet $100 to win $130.
ATSAgainst The Spread. Did they cover the point spread? 52.4% ATS is the break-even point at standard -110 odds.
B2BBack-to-Back. Team played a game yesterday. Historically costs 3-4 points of performance.

Power Rating

A 0–100 score blending win%, home/road record, and NBA net rating. Average team = ~50. The stat model uses this to find edges vs the Vegas line.
